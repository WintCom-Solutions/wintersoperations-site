issue #113's "not a scheduled automation" guidance — this is sent manually,
one time).

Subject: Quick check-in on the [offer name] delivery

cleanup/inventory snapshot/script] for about a week — has it been useful

  have the same documentation backlog.

Either way, thanks for being an early client on this.

[Name]
