# Channel Comparison — First Sales Experiment

Work package D of [#113](https://github.com/WintCom-Solutions/wintersoperations-site/issues/113).
Compares four acquisition channels for the first six-contact pilot batch
(see [`pilot-sequence.md`](./pilot-sequence.md)). Per #113's explicit
direction — "no paid ads, lead subscriptions, or new infrastructure for the
first experiment" — this comparison exists to justify prioritizing the
**$0 new-marketing-spend channels**, not to introduce a new paid one.

## Why direct MSP outreach is the $0 pilot channel

1. **Cost:** genuinely $0 — a public contact-form message costs nothing beyond Karl's time, unlike marketplace fees or (eventually) chamber membership dues.
2. **Fit:** the six prospects in `prospects.md` were chosen specifically because they are MSP/IT businesses whose public service descriptions make the documentation-cleanup pilot offer plausibly relevant — no other channel here lets us pre-select the buyer type this precisely.
3. **Measurability:** a batch of six known, named contacts with a shared outreach date produces a clean response-rate denominator (see `pilot-sequence.md`'s metrics) — LinkedIn and networking channels don't have a comparably bounded "batch" to measure against.
4. **No new infrastructure:** reuses the existing public contact routes already published by each prospect; no new tool, subscription, or account is required.

## Recommended role for the other three channels

- **LinkedIn:** update/create the Winters Operations presence (draft copy in
  [`linkedin-and-site-copy-proposals.md`](./linkedin-and-site-copy-proposals.md))
  and send a connection request alongside (not instead of) the direct
  outreach message to the same six prospects, so a reply on one channel
  doesn't get missed because the prospect only checks the other.
- **Local networking:** worth pursuing in parallel as a slower-building,
  referral-oriented channel (Hernando Main Street Chamber of Commerce is a
  real, currently-operating organization headquartered in the same town as
  Winters Operations) — but it is not part of this six-contact pilot's
  measured batch, since event-based relationship building doesn't produce a
  comparable "contacted → replied" data point on a fixed timeline.
- **Marketplace:** not activated for this pilot. If the direct-outreach and
  referral channels are exhausted without hitting the pilot's target
  customer count, revisit a specific marketplace's fee structure as a
  distinct, budgeted decision for Karl — not folded into the $0 experiment.
